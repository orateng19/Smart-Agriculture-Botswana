Welcome to Smart Agriculture

Smart Agriculture Botswana is a decision-support system designed to help farmers make informed agricultural decisions using data analysis, crop-yield predictions and agricultural guidance.

The system explores factors such as rainfall, temperature and historical crop production to help users understand agricultural patterns and estimate potential crop yields.

Our Mission

Support Farmers

Make agricultural information easier to understand and use when planning farming activities.

Use Data Effectively

Apply data analytics and machine learning to identify patterns in historical agricultural data.

Support Better Decisions

Present yield estimates and general farming guidance to support agricultural planning.

Why Smart Agriculture?

Agricultural production can be affected by changing rainfall, temperature and other environmental conditions. Data-driven tools can help farmers explore these factors and plan more effectively.

Our goal is to make agricultural data more accessible and demonstrate how machine learning can contribute to decision-support in Botswana.
